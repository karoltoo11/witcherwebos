Royal Gryphon

Class: Hybrids | Habitat: White Orchard, Skellige

Susceptibilities: 💨 Aard Sign • 💣 Grapeshot • 🗡️ Hybrid Oil

Griffins once dwelled solely in towering mountain peaks, but starvation has forced them down into populated valleys. They strike from the sky with terrifying speed, shredding prey with razor-sharp talons.

Witcher's Tactics: Use your crossbow to bring it crashing down mid-flight. Once grounded, ignite it with the Igni Sign or stagger it with Aard.

Ancient Leshen

Class: Relicts | Habitat: Primeval Forests, Velen

Susceptibilities: 🔥 Igni Sign • 💣 Dimeritium Bomb • 🗡️ Relict Oil

The Leshen is an ancient forest guardian fused with wood, roots, and bones. It commands forest beasts, summoning packs of wolves and murders of crows to do its bidding.

Witcher's Tactics: Made of living timber, the Leshen is highly vulnerable to fire. Burn it repeatedly with the Igni Sign and beware of subterranean roots bursting from the earth.

Noonwraith

Class: Specters | Habitat: Cornfields, Midday Sun

Susceptibilities: 🔮 Yrden Sign • 💣 Moon Dust • 🗡️ Specter Oil

Born from the violent demise of young maidens before their wedding day. They manifest at scorching midday among swaying wheat fields, blinding and tormenting wandering travelers.

Witcher's Tactics: In its immaterial form, the specter takes minimal damage from swords. Lure it into an Yrden magic circle to force it into material form, then strike with a silver blade coated in Specter Oil.

Werewolf

Class: Cursed Ones | Habitat: Deep Woods, Skellige, Near Settlements

Susceptibilities: 🔥 Igni Sign • 💣 Moon Dust • 🗡️ Cursed Oil

Werewolf transformation is an involuntary curse, wild and uncontrolled. Upon regaining human form, the afflicted retains no memory of their bloodthirsty deeds.

Witcher's Tactics: Werewolves possess formidable regenerative abilities. Suppress their healing with Moon Dust bombs or relentless Igni fire, keeping up fast strikes with a silver blade.

Fiend

Class: Relicts | Habitat: Swamps, Bogs, Ancient Ruins

Susceptibilities: 🔥 Igni Sign • 💣 Devil's Puffball • 🗡️ Relict Oil

A colossus of muscle and fur crowned with heavy antlers and a maw of razor-sharp fangs. It avoids humans when possible, but slaughters anyone in its path with brutal efficiency.

Witcher's Tactics: A fiend combines crushing brute strength with the hypnotic magic of its third eye. Throw a Samum or Devil's Puffball bomb when it prepares its trance, and dodge behind its flanks.

Ghoul

Class: Necrophages | Habitat: Graveyards, Crypts, Battlefields

Susceptibilities: 🔥 Igni Sign • ⛓️ Silver • 🗡️ Necrophage Oil

Vile scavengers that feed on the dead, digging up graves mere hours after burial. In packs, they embolden and attack living prey, shredding flesh with jagged claws.

Witcher's Tactics: Ghouls are terrified of fire and vulnerable to silver. Burn them with the Igni Sign and prioritize cutting down any commanding Alghul to scatter the pack.

Higher Vampire

Class: Vampires | Habitat: Duchy of Toussaint, Hidden Mansions

Susceptibilities: 🔥 Igni Sign • 💣 Devil's Puffball • 🗡️ Vampire Oil

Supernaturally gifted beings possessing human elegance and immense magical prowess. They drink blood not for sustenance, but as intoxicating wine. Only another higher vampire can grant them true death.

Witcher's Tactics: Never allow it to vanish into mist. Disperse its mist form with the Igni Sign, quaff Black Blood before engagement, and strike with swift flurries of silver coated in Vampire Oil.

Basilisk

Class: Draconids | Habitat: Caverns, Damp Cellars, Badlands

Susceptibilities: 💨 Aard Sign • 🧪 Golden Oriole • 🗡️ Draconid Oil

Contrary to popular folklore, a basilisk does not turn victims to stone with its gaze; it spews lethal acid and deadly neurotoxins. It boasts an avian beak and reptilian wings tipped with venomous barbs.

Witcher's Tactics: Drink a Golden Oriole potion before the duel to turn its venom harmless. Blast it out of the air or interrupt its charge with the Aard Sign, and stay clear of its whip-like tail.

Grave Hag

Class: Necrophages | Habitat: Graveyards, Necropolises after Dark

Susceptibilities: 🔮 Yrden Sign • 🛡️ Quen Sign • 🗡️ Necrophage Oil

A grotesque necrophage resembling a withered, hunched crone. It cracks open tombstones and slurps marrow using an impossibly long, prehensile tongue that doubles as a deadly whip in combat.

Witcher's Tactics: Keep your Quen shield active to absorb sudden whiplashes from its tongue. When it lashes out, sever the tongue with a well-timed parry or trap the hag inside an Yrden circle.
